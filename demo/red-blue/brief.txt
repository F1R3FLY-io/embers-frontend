Decision: Introduce a practical code-review policy for six pull requests per day.
Constraints: Four engineers can each spend 60 minutes per day reviewing, for a maximum of 240 reviewer-minutes per day. Each independent review takes an estimated 30 minutes. No new tooling budget. Urgent production fixes must retain an emergency path. Use existing pull requests and CI.
Initial proposal to challenge: Require two independent approvals before every merge, including emergencies. Compare that demand with the available review capacity and consider one approval with CI checks.
Deliverable: Recommend a practical policy, explain changes after red-team review, preserve unresolved risks, and give a short rollout checklist with responsible roles, success measures and rollback.
Unknowns: The stated review times are planning estimates, not observed measurements. We have not measured review turnaround time or emergency-fix frequency. Do not invent these measurements.
